# Changelog

## 3.5.2

- Show for players: when the recommended turn moves you or aims an area, Show draws the route on your map, as it does on the GM's card. Only you see it. The route goes round only the reactions the party has seen this fight. It clears when the turn passes or the window closes.
- Each turn starts with only Your Turn open. Worth Knowing, Around You and Foes start folded; one you open stays open for the rest of that turn.
- Your Turn has a green edge (the GM's card keeps red), and the action pips and bars on the helper match.

## 3.5.1

Finishes the 3.5 coverage work.

- Staves prepared with PF2e Dailies: their spells are read with the staff's DC and attack, cost charges equal to their rank (a staff's cantrips cost none, and a staff not in hand takes an Interact first), and spell thrift weighs each by how many uses of that rank the charges left pay for.
- A temporary scroll made at the day's preparations (Scroll Adept, through PF2e Dailies) is held back only lightly by spell thrift, since it's gone tomorrow anyway.
- With no damaging cantrip prepared, spell thrift weighed a slot spell against the best other plan, which could be a focus spell or Untamed Form that spends a Focus Point. It now weighs it against your best turn that spends nothing at all (usually your Strikes), and says so ("no damaging cantrip is prepared").

## 3.5.0

**Companions and summons, for every class**
- An animal companion is planned for whatever class has one (a ranger's wolf, a champion's steed, a beastmaster's companion), not only a druid's: Command an Animal with the companion's own Strikes from its sheet, then your best two actions at the same foe, as one turn. A mature companion's free action is counted against the command; Side by Side flanks.
- A summoned creature's Sustain is planned the same way (its two actions, then your two), and any caster with a summon spell gets the summon cast beside a foe, marked for spell thrift. A familiar gets a note.
- A minion is yours when it names you as its master, or else when the same player owns it; another character's companion is left alone.

**Scrolls and wands**
- Spell thrift weighs a scroll or a wand like a slot of its rank with one use left, so the helper doesn't read a scroll or spend the wand's daily use for a small gain over a cantrip, and the plan says which it is.

**Archetypes**
- Checked with four multiclass characters (a rogue with Wizard Dedication, a champion with Fighter Dedication, a cleric with Rogue Dedication, a fighter with Beastmaster Dedication): each dedication brings in its class's read, and all of them are in the test set of real sheets.

## 3.4.1

- When a combat ends, each character's record of that fight (Spellstrike's charge, the foe exploited, who it Demoralized...) is cleared from the character too, as the combat's own record already was. Nothing about a fight outlives it.
- The class reads share their small helpers (ability modifiers, class DC, weapon dice, slots left, "twice"/"three times" and the like) from one place instead of a dozen copies. Nothing they say changed: every test and the full advice for thirty test characters came out the same before and after.

## 3.4.0

**The Turn Helper is turn advice, not a second character sheet**
- The card is shorter: Your Turn (the recommended turn, with the other options folded under it), Worth Knowing (the three class notes that bear most on this turn, the rest folded), Around You (who can reach you, flanking, provoking, nearby cover) and Foes. Your HP, AC, saves, Speed and the Strikes list are left to the sheet.

**What you can see on a foe**
- The conditions on a foe and the effects showing on its token now count: frightened 2 takes 2 off its estimated AC and every save, off-guard 2 off its AC, and the Foes list shows them with their values ("frightened 2", "Bane", "-2 AC from what shows on it").
- The party's learned AC and saves are kept net of what showed on the foe when each roll was made (and of the off-guard a flanked roll carried), so they still hold when the condition wears off.

**What the helper remembers**
- What the party has seen each foe do (a reaction, an attack outside its own turn, a spell, a ranged attack, a heal) is kept on the combat by the GM's client: every player's helper shares it, and a reload no longer loses it.
- Your own character's fight is remembered from your chat cards: Spellstrike charged or spent (with a button on the helper to set it by hand), the foe your thaumaturge exploited, the chalice drained, the wand boosted, and the foes you've Demoralized or asked a Pointed Question (not offered again for 10 minutes). The magus and thaumaturge plans no longer have to guess.

## 3.3.1

A second-reader review of 3.3.0: six reviewers who hadn't seen the code checked every class read against the PF2e 8.6.1 compendium text and the shared layers for leaks and logic, then each finding was fixed and re-checked against the packs. 31 High and 62 Medium findings fixed, most Lows too.

**The Turn Helper and what a player sees**
- Foes carry nothing from their sheets: their senses, languages, class, spell list, ranged attacks and feats such as All-Around Vision no longer shape the advice. A foe counts as a caster, a healer or a shooter once the party has seen it cast, heal or shoot this fight; a reaction counts once seen (an attack roll a foe makes outside its own turn now counts as its Reactive Strike).
- A condition the GM sets on a foe (concealed, hidden, undetected) holds even when PF2e Visioner says otherwise; a hidden foe gets its DC 11 flat check and is no longer the recommended target.
- Off-guard foes (prone, grabbed and the rest) are 2 easier to hit in the estimates; marking one off-guard twice no longer counts the −2 twice.
- The party's learned AC bounds were wrong after a natural 20, a natural 1 or a critical failure; corrected (the NPC card's guesses use the same bounds).
- Undetected foes no longer bend the helper's paths around their square, and on gridless scenes the helper no longer warns about reactions the party hasn't seen.
- A badly hurt character is no longer told to "Break off" as if it were an NPC; a character that can't reach a flier advances on a foe it can reach instead.
- Sneak Attack dice go only on agile, finesse and ranged Strikes (and a ruffian's simple and smaller weapons), not on a greatsword.
- Spell thrift covers class plans that spend a slot (Quickened Casting, hex and Force Bolt follow-ups, summons, Channel Smite, Psi Burst after a spell); with no cantrip, a slot spell is weighed against your best turn that spends nothing; innate spells aren't taxed; flexible casters' slots are counted right.
- Battle Medicine picks the DC that heals most on average, can bring up a dying ally, and only your own Battle Medicine's immunity stops it.

**Every class**
- Feats with an apostrophe (Implement's Assault, Magus's Analysis, Deity's Protection, Archwizard's Might, Executioner's Volley and more) are found on real sheets; some had vanished from the advice entirely.
- Each class's read also turns on for its dedication.
- Rules corrections across the reads, among them: Fling Magic's dice, Rallying Banner's healing, Mercy's conditions by mercy and level, champion numbers that use half your level, the oracle's curse stages, Thrall Charge's 30-foot range, the kineticist's blast range and fire junction, Seething Frenzy, Feral Swing and Giant-Felling Comet as basic saves, Mega Bomb's area, Esvadir's bleed, Cruonign's scaling, Exult in Violence, Hero's Defiance, Crushing Ground and Cornucopia.
- Turns that were illegal or wasted are gone: Two-Weapon Flurry more than once a turn, a free Rage from Quick-Tempered after initiative, Rage damage while fatigued, Violent Unleash after you've acted, four eidolon actions in three, Overdrive twice in a round, Gang Up's off-guard on ranged shots, a melee Strike at a foe 25 feet away, daily tactics and banners already spent, more than one animist primary apparition, guardian shield bashes the feats don't allow.
- New: the alchemist's free Quick Vial every turn; the magus plans both a Spellstrike turn and a recharge turn and says which assumes a charge (PF2e doesn't track it); the animist's primary apparition can be set with the `primaryApparition` flag.

Checked with every class's own tests (56), all 30 test characters' real sheets, the NPC turn replay (unchanged), and every class on the dev arena.

## 3.3.0

- Your Class covers all twenty-nine classes at every level 1 to 20: Psychic, Summoner, Kineticist, Gunslinger, Inventor, Animist, Exemplar, Guardian, Necromancer and Runesmith are new (each read from the system's class features and feats, Remaster names first; the eidolon's and construct's Strikes come from their own sheets, the exemplar's spark from the sheet's roll options, runes by name since PF2e ships no rune items).
- Spell thrift: slot spells are recommended only when they beat your best cantrip by a margin that grows with the slot's rank and shrinks with the slots left at that rank, halved when the spell is decisive (two foes caught, a foe it can drop); cantrips, focus spells, heals and buffs are never held back. The plan says the numbers; a per-player setting ("Turn Helper: spell thrift") makes it frugal, normal or free-spending.
- Cantrips heighten from their own base rank (a psychic's unique cantrips start at 3 or 5), and the board marks focus spells as such.
- The advisor's text reading steps aside for the actions the new reads plan (the finishers and blasts, impulses, deeds, transcendences, grave spells and so on), so nothing is listed twice.
- Class reads can be turned on by more than one feature slug, so a dedication's own grants (a psychic's conscious mind, a gunslinger's way) reach the read.
- Firearms and crossbows: a weapon's reload is read from its reload field (PF2e doesn't carry it as a trait), so the advisor no longer plans three shots a turn with a reload 1 gun. With PF2e Ranged Combat 8 the loaded round is read from the weapon's sub-items, and a gun the module knows is empty opens the advisor's own shooting plan with the reload.
- The inventor's weapon innovation is taken from the sheet's roll options (a shield is never mistaken for it, and an innovation left in the pack is said so); a runesmith who Traces at range only Strikes once in reach, closing first when the actions allow.
- Checked on the dev world with a scratch character of each of the ten new classes beside five foes (a summoner with its eidolon; a kineticist built through the gate prompts).

## 1.8.0

- Step, then Stride: standing in someone's reach, it now plans a Step out of reach before the Stride, so an approach, a retreat or a Break off no longer walks through a Reactive Strike when five feet would have avoided it.
- Dropping a foe is weighed by the dice, not the average: the chance the plan's Strikes take them down ("likely drops", "could drop", "might drop"), damage past their HP counts half, and taking someone out of the fight is worth about two of its own hits.
- Conditions are valued on the same scale as hits (slowed or stunned about a hit, paralyzed two), at every level, so Fear, Slow and knockdowns compete with Strikes instead of losing to them as the levels climb.
- Flying up costs double (it's difficult terrain) and straight down half; "Fly up N ft" fits one action only within half its fly Speed.
- "Out of their reach" counts archers and casters: a foe who can shoot or cast at a square counts half as much as one who can reach it, and the fly off step says who can still shoot it.
- Break off is offered second, not first, to a creature two or more levels over the party or the last of its side, with a note that it's your call.
- A Shove off a ledge no longer charges the foe's fall damage to the shover.
- Flanking's -2 AC isn't applied to a ranged Strike variant.
- Other options show what they're worth here when that differs from their damage ("about 19.5, worth 7.7 here after 8.4 of risk"), so the recommendation never looks beaten by its own alternative.
- What the NPCs learn of a foe's AC is now read from the rolls: each hit bounds the AC from above and each miss from below (crits and critical misses by ten more), and the guessed AC is squeezed inside the bounds.
- Damage undone from its card takes its threat back.
- An encounter-wide trigger ("two wolves are down") whispers once, naming everyone it moves; the fallen aren't counted.
- PF2e's simple action cards (Stand, Take Cover, Raise a Shield, Interact, Step, Stride...) count in the turn so far by their glyph.
- The drawn path is cleared when the window closes, the combat ends or the token is deleted.
- The window re-plans only for changes the board reads (HP, conditions, positions, alliance), not every edit to an actor on the scene.

## 1.7.3

Hidden and undetected carry to every square the creature might move to; from its own square Visioner's answer stands. The Board shows what Visioner says. Without Visioner nothing changes. No dependency: it is read only if present.
- Mobility (NPC Core): a move of half its Speed or less doesn't trigger reactions; longer moves do. It was read as never provoking.

- A trigger line with two conditions ("X is down while Y is still on the bridge", "one tough is down and the other is hurt") is left to the GM to watch; it used to fire on the first clause alone. "Is out of reach" is no longer read as "is down", nor "is taken hostage".
- An airborne animal with an area ability no longer loses its whole plan to an error (the card said it could reach no one).
- Drinking a potion or using an elixir counts as two actions in the turn so far unless it was already in hand (draw, then use); - takes one back.

## 1.7.0

- Height: elevation counts for reach, range, areas and Reactive Strike. Fliers come down to fight and fly up out of reach by a stated height, and stay up only by Flying each turn. Walkers drop off ledges or climb inclines (PF2e Terrains) to reach a foe. Regions are read at the creature's height.

- Reviving a fallen ally is worth far more than healing HP, for creatures that think.

## 1.6.0

- Threat works like aggro: a percentage on top of what an attack is worth, so it still matters at level 20 (before, its fixed few points were lost in high-level damage). Cleverer creatures weigh it more, and notice who hurts their allies.
- Hit and run for clever fliers, and areas weigh where the creature ends up.
- Monster Core recharges ("[[/gmr 1d4 #Recharge]]") are rolled, once per use; Draconic Momentum's critical hit recharges the breath.
- A flight counts as Fly actions, not Strides; fliers can plan up to 240 feet.
- Captive Rake and other abilities that need a grabbed foe; Double Bite; Draconic Frenzy's three Strikes; Spore Cloud as an area; more ways of writing the multiple attack penalty.
- Much faster for big fliers with many area spells (an ancient spellcaster dragon went from 14 seconds to under one).
- Fixes from a second review: a held creature keeps its Swallow Whole and similar plans; area plans pay for the Reactive Strikes they walk through at what those Strikes are worth; exposure counts only foes that can act.
